Why it works

Built to be harder
to work around.

🌐
Harder to work around
Most parental controls live on the device — and kids know how to get around them. LatSync works at a deeper level, so switching browsers, using a different app, or turning on a VPN does not help.
🔔
You know if something changes
If your child tries to remove the app or change settings, you get a notification right away. Every change is time-stamped so you always have the full picture.
🔒
Simple family setup
Open the app on your phone first, then install it on your child's device while you're both at home. No account creation. No codes to share. The two devices connect automatically.
👤
No messages or history read
LatSync only controls which apps and websites your child can reach. It never reads their messages, photos, or browsing history. Protection without surveillance.

Product direction

More than
blocking.

As major platforms introduce safer experiences for kids and teens, LatSync is designed to help families guide children toward the right version of those platforms — not just block access outright.

That means helping families steer children toward safer, age-appropriate experiences when platforms provide them — instead of forcing parents to choose between total access and total blocking.

🗺️ On the product roadmap
🎮
Gaming with guardrails
Guide children toward age-appropriate gaming experiences as platforms expand their own safety controls for younger players.
📺
Video built for their age
As video platforms develop dedicated younger experiences, LatSync is designed to help families navigate toward those safer modes rather than blocking outright.
💬
Social with supervision
Regulatory changes are pushing platforms to build safer teen experiences. LatSync is designed to work with those improvements, not around them.
Designed for families

Full protection for
your whole family.

From online protection for children to one-tap emergency help for the people you love, LatSync is committed to keeping core family safety free for families who need it most.

🔐
Private by design
LatSync never reads messages, photos, or browsing history. It manages which apps and websites are accessible — nothing more.
🏠
Your family's data stays under your control
Settings and logs live on your devices, not on our servers. We do not collect, analyze, or sell your family's information.
🤝
Builds trust over time
As children get older, Guardians can loosen rules easily. LatSync supports your parenting — it does not replace the conversation.
📵
Covers every device at home
Phones, tablets, gaming consoles, smart TVs. Rules apply to every device on your home network — not just the ones with an app installed.
⚡
Reliable and offline-ready
LatSync runs locally on your home network. Core protection keeps working even if your internet connection goes down.
🆘
Emergency help for everyone
Every family member — children, parents, grandparents — gets a one-tap help button that sends live GPS to your trusted contacts. No monthly fee. No call center. The right people know immediately.
